The artist

Celestine Tan is a self-taught Malaysian-Singaporean artist. He works in ink and watercolour, sketching on location wherever he travels to. He is currently based in Zurich, Switzerland.

Zurich

Nine sketches from the city and canton where Celestine lives: the lido, the river, the old town and the forest edge.

Across Switzerland

Eight sketches from trips across six cantons: Basel-Stadt, Bern, Lucerne, Schaffhausen, Schwyz and Graubünden. From the Rhine Falls to an abbey in Val Müstair.
