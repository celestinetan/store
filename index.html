The Artist

Hi there! Thank you for stopping by! I’m Celestine—a self-taught, Malaysian-born Singaporean artist now living in Switzerland. I love travelling, spending time outdoors, and sketching the world as I see it, working primarily in ink and watercolour. Each piece in this gallery captures a moment or place from my journeys. I hope you enjoy exploring my work—and perhaps find something to bring into your own space.

Warmly,

Zurich

Across Switzerland

Eight sketches from trips across six cantons: Basel-Stadt, Bern, Lucerne, Schaffhausen, Schwyz and Graubünden. From the Rhine Falls to an abbey in Val Müstair.
