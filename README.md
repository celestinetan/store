# Switzerland, live sketched: art prints by Celestine Tan

The gallery page for Celestine Tan's art prints: 17 ink and watercolour sketches drawn outdoors across Switzerland in 2026.

**Live:** https://store.celestinetan.com/

> Prints are sold and fulfilled through creativehub and theprintspace. Their terms apply to every order. See [Disclaimer & Terms of Use](#disclaimer--terms-of-use).

## What it does
1. Shows the works framed in oak, hung in two rooms: Zurich and Across Switzerland.
2. Each work links to its print page on creativehub, where buyers choose a size and frame and check out.
3. Lists paper, sizes, framing and delivery in one place.

## Known limits
- Prices on this page are copied by hand from creativehub. creativehub's checkout is always the final price.
- Delivery cost is shown at checkout, based on the buyer's country.
